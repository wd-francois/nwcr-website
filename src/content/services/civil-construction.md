Our civil and construction division provides reliable, high-quality services across Perth and regional Western Australia. With strong local knowledge, a skilled Indigenous workforce and a commitment to safety, we support mining and infrastructure projects from site preparation through to construction and remediation.

Each project is delivered with a focus on quality, efficiency and safety, and on creating meaningful employment opportunities in the Pilbara.
