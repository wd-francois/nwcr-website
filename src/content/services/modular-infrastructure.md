We build with EPS-FR modular panels: a smarter, faster way to deliver residential, commercial and mine-site buildings that are scalable, compliant and built to perform.

Our panels are precision-engineered and manufactured locally using high-performance materials for strength and thermal efficiency. They are delivered and assembled by our trained team, **cutting build times by up to 50%**.

From concept to completion, we plan each build around your needs, site conditions and compliance requirements.
