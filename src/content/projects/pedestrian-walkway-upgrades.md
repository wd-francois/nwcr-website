NWCR delivered walkway upgrades at a Rio Tinto rail yard. The works included:

- site clearing and grubbing
- bulk earthworks to construct the subgrade
- installation of three 12 m CSP drainage culverts
- erosion control to batters using geofabric and rock protection
- a reinforced 32 MPa concrete walkway with precast edge beams and panels
- handrails, wire fencing, maze barriers and safety signage at rail crossings.

All works were carried out under track access constraints with lookout protection. A variation added 42 m of footpath to the site office.
