NWCR removed the bridge's existing plastic grating driving surface and replaced it with a new, approved True Grid system.

To protect the pedestrian handrail, we installed new painted steel kerb sections along the length of the bridge. Together with a speed restriction reduced from 20 km/h to 10 km/h, the upgrade allows vehicles of up to 6,400 kg GVM to cross the bridge.

We also installed approach kerbing at the bridge abutments to prevent vehicle access to the East Walkway.
