From 2021 to 2023, NWCR fabricated structural components — including grid mesh and handrails — and erected 30% of the structural steel and 70% of the mechanical equipment for the Western Animal Nutrition feedmill plant.

The work was led by NWCR's on-site management team, using a structured construction method with structural hold points and survey for the mechanical installation. Our QA/QC Manager documented each stage into a deliverable MDR package.
